# Post-PR51 Wholesome and Singular audit

Baseline: `9b0c3318224be4353cf9243928f8267790b963fa` from `JeofW/cbp` master. This audit uses a separate worktree and preserves the former PR51 checkout and its two edited handoffs. Production was treated as evidence during implementation. Release and deployment receipts identify the subsequently published source and exact installed artifacts.

The original request covers the complete quest/combat process, not just the reported log strings. This report distinguishes reproducible defects, deliberately retained behavior, and the limits of offline validation. Raw production logs, character settings and copied client assets stay outside the repository.

## Root-cause changes

**1. Decode the actual original-client Spell record.** `WoWDb` now copies/decodes 680 bytes rather than 704. Read-only IDA analysis of the build-12340 executable shows `0x2A8` at native readers `0x4CFD20` and `0x61DC30`. Reading 24 extra bytes makes otherwise valid packed records depend on the following record. Boundary regressions poison those following bytes, reject oversized requested records, and verify the raw-record boundary. Unknown aura observations remain unknown; the repair does not pretend missing metadata means a buff is absent.

**2. Release obsolete quest attempts after revoking execution permission.** A current refresh first invalidates published work, then abandons only its exact old owned attempt before constructing the next schedule. This prevents the scheduler from excluding its own quest. A newer generation for the same quest remains protected, and an obsolete refresh lease cannot release current ownership. Existing completion, publication, activation and callback protections remain enabled.

**3. Distinguish normal loot closure from a failed request.** The loot owner snapshots the observed slot count. After it submits the final slot, disappearance of that exact frame can complete processing without closing an unrelated frame. A replacement nonzero frame GUID never inherits authority. Separate messages identify failure to open a loot window and changes during slot processing. Submission still does not prove that every item arrived in the bags; existing later observations and bounded recovery retain that responsibility.

**4. Retry actual rest admission while paused.** Wholesome stops movement before requesting use and revisits the current consumable observation during the pause. Core rest retains its bounded per-kind timers, actor/world fences and legacy public void APIs; new boolean entrypoints report submission success. Failed use no longer produces an “ate/drank” claim. Fresh valid inventory clears obsolete absence flags. A stopped/restarted session cannot inherit the previous rest pause. Flying, transport, water, combat and invalid actors remain excluded from ordinary consumption.

**5. Observe trained riding and use the flight provider.** Flight eligibility reads the original riding skill descriptor: 225 or above, with a real available flight provider and client-area permission. It retains the original Outland/Northrend map rules, Northrend Cold Weather Flying, level, outdoor, water, transport and vehicle restrictions. Actor/map changes during the area query invalidate its result. Localized skill metadata cannot hide a valid descriptor skill ID. Eligible hotspot travel reaches Flightor; existing ground fallback remains available. Already-airborne navigation is not forced into ground movement by a transient mount-eligibility failure.

**6. Preserve mounted travel until escape actually fails.** The running quest and mount owner now share progress-based admission. Healthy positional and route progress tolerates passing aggro and brief navigation pauses. Root/stun, critical health, rapid health deterioration, absent destination, three seconds without positional progress or six seconds without route progress yield to combat. These durations and health thresholds are conservative recovery policy, not client mechanics. Moving into a wall cannot suppress combat indefinitely. Airborne admission never voluntarily drops the rider. Forced dismount immediately restores ground combat, after which the existing travel/mount path can resume. Hotspot proximity requires actual profile pull intent, not merely an incidental target.

**7. Handle sparse item effects and incomplete inventory metadata.** Item effect slots are a fixed five-slot collection, not a list terminated by the first empty slot. Consumable discovery ignores invalid/exhausted items, handles missing item metadata and world changes, and reevaluates current bags. An unknown secondary effect cannot prove that an item has no specialty effect. Level checks, quality/stack selection, specialty exclusions and the distinction between food/drink and potions remain intact. Native item-use ownership and cooldown admission are retained.

**8. Validate talent identities before spending points.** Talented validates the entire template against exact live names, tree, ranks, maxima, tier and prerequisite observations. It resolves indices from current names; an exported index cannot authorize a different talent. The original-client request checks character GUID, active talent group, live talent identity, unspent points and the absence of someone else's preview in one Lua evaluation. It submits one point and waits for a learned-rank observation before another. It never commits or resets an unrelated preview. Disabled/stopped/changed-character or changed-spec events cannot allocate points. The replacement Ret template contains 71 original-client points, with milestone ordering that reaches Art of War at 40/41, Crusader Strike at 50 and Divine Storm at 60 from an initially empty valid leveling allocation. Existing character talents are not reset or migrated automatically.

**9. Refresh idle observations without weakening quarantine.** An empty timed-idle schedule can refresh observations after the existing ten-second scan cooldown even when an unrelated endpoint is quarantined for much longer. Refreshing observations does not grant that endpoint permission to execute. Successful active work is not periodically interrupted by this idle timer. The existing completed-quest history cache and server/UI delays still affect when new authoritative data becomes available. Status output retains exclusion counts and representative examples without repeating a world-wide exclusion list every pulse.

**10. Respect both Art of War ranks and Holy Wrath's resource cost.** Rank-one proc 53489 removes 750 ms of cast time; rank-two proc 59578 removes 100%. Their shared name does not prove an instant cast. Rank one is a stationary lower-priority filler; rank two can use the instant opportunities already present in the rotation. Expired or unknown same-name auras cannot authorize moving casts. Holy Wrath remains available against eligible Undead/Demons, but its damage-only filler respects the configured mana recovery reserve. Health at or below 70% retains its stun utility. Mounted travel is excluded. The separate [Ret policy analysis](RET_335A_POLICY_ANALYSIS.md) explains why the Consecration threshold and general Exorcism restrictions were retained.

## Scope of proof and retained limitations

The proofs include real-owner C# regressions, complete runtime-source compilation against the built host, stock Lua 5.1 execution of generated requests, original-client numeric evidence, and a deterministic sensitivity model. They do not establish measured in-game DPS, successful completion of every quest, exact behavior of every private-server customization or an observed post-deployment questing session. The new diagnostics and exact artifact receipts make those live checks attributable.

The native navigation library is preserved. Dense ranged-pull isolation is not asserted as validated, and its existing acceptance gate remains disabled. Automatic talent repair applies only to future eligible unspent points; the existing allocation may still require a deliberate user respec. Model comparisons do not silently replace manual seal/Judgement assignments or group buff protection.
