## Reading the classifications

**V — verified and preserved:** retained controlled/source coverage, exercised by the current suite where applicable. **R — demonstrated defect repaired:** actual behavioral red followed by minimal source repair and passing controlled tests. **L — source verified with acceptance limitation:** the decision path is established, but controlled leaves or source evidence cannot prove the live outcome. **D — exact external dependency:** the necessary input is named below. **U — intentionally unsupported/fail-closed:** missing provenance/protocol prevents enabling a behavior. Several classifications can apply to one requirement because a verified managed decision does not complete physical/native/server acceptance.

Evidence anchors: LOCAL17 is W110_FINAL_PASS_COMBATROAMFINAL_LOCAL_GATE_20260929 (17/17 plus host, 1900 stable inputs); OPTIMIZED is W110_COMBAT_ROAM_OPTIMIZED_20260929 (whole Wholesome and identical group membership/binaries); HOSTED17 is the exact-source archive in W110_COMBAT_ROAM_EVIDENCE.json. Five distinct repair intervals preserve the recovered pre-pull work and add combat, roaming-tail and guard-cleanup fixes. Earlier high-risk/native/CLR evidence remains at its original source. Controlled results do not prove live outcomes.
