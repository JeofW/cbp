## Causes and changes

**Mounted retaliation.** At 16:04:31.848 the log reports a dismount request; at 16:04:32.353 it observes ground and unmounted state. Subsequent combat ticks repeatedly create new ground-ready transition owners despite the completed handoff. The repair uses complete current unmounted admission before constructing a new landing owner. A retained airborne or pending dismount owner still completes its original transition. The fast path binds actor, process, run, target, POI and input ownership; callbacks cannot transfer a failed observation to a replacement session. Paladin combat aura maintenance yields its first melee-range opportunity to the ordinary guarded attack rotation, then resumes once autoattack is observed. No attack permission is inferred from arrival alone.

**Healing continuation.** Retribution and Protection supplied a start predicate, including `!IsCasting`, to the shared `Spell.Heal` continuation. An admitted heal could consequently cancel itself as soon as its own casting state became visible. Start and continuation conditions are now distinct. Ret retains its existing solo and group combat thresholds, while Protection hard casts remain out of combat. The actual shared Heal and TreeSharp continuations are tested for completion, recovery cancellation and replacement-actor isolation.

**Food ownership and paused recovery.** An in-flight health action could reject food after the food retry interval was already spent. A cheap shared recovery-admission check now precedes inventory work and timer expenditure; the final item reservation and native-entry guards still enforce current ownership. Diagnostics distinguish a request from actual eating/drinking. Wholesome's active pause previously retried only below its lower start thresholds even though the pause waited for higher resume thresholds; it now retries absent food/drink activity until the configured resume goal. This preserves the existing start/resume settings and immediate-threat/loot policy. An additional test exposed lazy profile loading from recovery ownership checks; these now use the existing pure `CurrentProfileSnapshot` accessor.

**Quest 10294 acquisition.** The optional incidental-travel candidate called `MyAggroRange`, whose unavailable aura metadata could invalidate the entire Targeting publication before the required quest-source include callback ran. The log repeatedly identifies missing metadata for aura 61988 while the quest waits without a selected live source. Only that optional route observation is contained: it cannot authorize an incidental pull, and it cannot erase independently selected quest targets. Required unknown hostility still invalidates target publication. A zero-length route no longer reads aggro modifiers.

**Consecration and adjacent area decisions.** A decision for an eight-yard effect used the forty-yard hostile population, and its dungeon safety scan evaluated reaction before radius. Unrelated distant objects could therefore suppress or delay an otherwise valid local area decision. Consecration now uses radius-scoped hostility observations; geometry precedes reaction/engagement work. The safety area for Consecration, Divine Storm and Holy Wrath is centered on the actor. Relevant unknown observations and unengaged enemies inside the effect still deny admission. The same radius-first query serves nearby seal, Avenging Wrath and Holy Wrath decisions. Existing resource, learned-spell, cooldown, movement and count conditions remain.

**Control auras and emergency support.** The log contains 281 null-reference exceptions in the crowd-control reader. Missing harmful aura metadata is now a typed unavailable observation. Unlearned racials do not evaluate expensive control/world requirements, and harmful-control readers do not require unrelated helpful metadata. Holy/Protection self emergency restrictions use the complete raw numeric restriction IDs, matching Ret's existing contract. Protection Lay on Hands uses the healing path instead of expecting a buff acknowledgement; Divine Protection retains exact supported-aura coverage. Required unknown coverage and Forbearance/restriction IDs remain enforced.
