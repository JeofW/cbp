## Implementation

`MountedCombatTransition` now treats explicit Kill/pull intent as the authority to land for combat. Incidental combat flags, damage, health, roots, stuns and stalled travel do not create that intent. After an observed forced dismount, falling and unavailable ground observations still block attack; separately observed supported ground releases ordinary combat. A remount revokes a pending protective transition. The contradictory legacy `Mount.ShouldDismount` escalation and unused `MountedTravelProgress` implementation were removed.

The existing explicit hotspot-target admission remains: a profile's matching MobID can request a pull even with the default unlimited maximum level, while an unrelated nearby target cannot authorize a dismount. Actual target selection, committed POI identity and shared ground transition tests are retained. Reaching a hotspot, setting a target or submitting a dismount is still insufficient evidence of a completed ground handoff.

`GroundTransitionMachine` preserves an incremental flight search across pulses. Supported actors can use ground fallback after exhaustion or a planning budget of eight batches/two monotonic seconds. Airborne landing protections remain intact. The budget limits subsequent queries; it cannot interrupt a single slow native call. It does not promise that flying is always selected in unsupported, indoor, invalid-mesh or uneconomic conditions.

`GroundLootApproach` now retains object selection and bounded retry/rescan only. Its separate raw flight, descent, mount-removal and global-navigation-reset commands were removed. The same `GroundTransition` owner used by other interactions owns movement, supported landing, a pending removal request and observed unmount readiness. Cancellation releases that owner's effects without clearing successor navigation.

GameObject model origins are not necessarily walkable points. The geometry search uses observed support within the object's actual interaction volume or a complete mesh route to an observed ground endpoint within that volume. It retains footprint, body, roof, liquid, complete-path and identity checks. A ray into an opaque object model is no longer an interaction veto. NPC line-of-sight checks remain in place.

Both generic loot and direct typed-objective callers use `GroundTransition.TryInteractWith`. Current native object usability is read before preparing the effect; exact actor, run, session, subject, position and caller admission are checked at submission. Prepared guards do not execute collision, Lua or native usability queries. A request is not a loot or quest acknowledgement: matching loot-source events, slot delivery and typed objective/inventory observations retain their separate owners and bounded waits.

Original-client facts are retained in `Tools/EvidenceAudit/fixtures/ret_335a_mechanics.json`, bound to Spell.dbc SHA256 `d5cce1a83550dcfa9eb2f0251dbb11fd24c272534b2b1a9b230924a44d817ab3`. The retained AzerothCore coefficient reference is `8337a378ac325e62a6a91e00c6a5e944205e8536:data/sql/base/db_world/spell_bonus_data.sql`, with 0.04 attack-power and 0.04 spell-power contribution per tick. The actual rotation still prioritizes its emergency/resource actions, appropriate Exorcism/Hammer of Wrath opportunities, Crusader Strike, Divine Storm and Judgement before this filler. Selection requires a learned spell, valid actor/target, melee proximity, stationary actor and target, no cast/channel, a configured pack count or sustained boss, and a recovery mana reserve. Final spell admission retains actual current cost/cooldown/target/GCD checks. A new cheap cooldown/GCD prefilter avoids querying reactions, engagement and auras for an unavailable ground spell. Invalid or non-finite mana/configuration cannot authorize it.

The previously dungeon-only extra-pack check now protects Consecration in ordinary questing too, at selection and at string/numeric spell dispatch. Current observed engagement is required for every relevant hostile inside the patch. Protected harmful controls such as Polymorph, Sap, sleep, shackle, incapacitates, fear/turn, banish and frozen effects veto placement. A stun remains eligible. Unavailable relevant harmful-aura coverage remains unavailable; unrelated beneficial metadata and units outside the effect do not consume control/engagement checks.

The fresh deterministic study retained the explicit 1,440 encounter scenarios and five policies (7,200 policy runs), plus 250 dwell/lifetime sensitivity cases. It uses synthetic gear, predictable procs, nominal tick timing and simplified combat; it is not a client/server simulator or measured DPS. Lowering the threshold to two made 29 paired fights faster and three slower, but improved only 18 kill-plus-refill cycles and worsened 15. The six-second short-pack heuristic changed no matched outcomes in this particular matrix. A count alone cannot predict useful damage: six enemies lasting one tick supply six target-ticks; two lasting eight supply sixteen. The evidence supports safer admission and reduced unnecessary work, not a universal threshold or speculative TTK optimizer. No priority shuffle or invented live lifetime estimator was introduced.
