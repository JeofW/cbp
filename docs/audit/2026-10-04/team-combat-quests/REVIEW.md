# Team combat, quest obligations and responsive travel

The October 4 audit fixes shared execution paths for moving quest NPCs, required mounted combat, ordinary grinding, instance combat recipients, preferred flight departure, and aerial route avoidance. Baseline: merged master `85714dc9935c7c4bb8b59f9b21bc24ee5888d647`. Original client: WoW 3.3.5a build 12340. Source evidence remains pinned to the existing TrinityCore/TDB335 identities.

## Reported failures and repaired behavior

**Mahuram Stouthoof.** The complete production log eventually records interaction at 12:28:25, after approximately 52 seconds of unmounted pursuit. The prior readiness test required exactly unchanged positions across native observations and did not admit a usable final approach while the actor was moving. GroundTransition now distinguishes preparation from submission: approach continuously, stop once when range and sight are usable, observe the stop, and revalidate the same NPC before interaction. A walking NPC may advance within the bounded observed region; replacement wrappers, loss of range, missing sight and moving actor submission remain denied. Pickup, turn-in and service consumers share this path.

**Required mobs and hotspot movement.** Previously, mounted travel suppressed ordinary target acquisition, and a Running travel child could keep the quest root until its destination. An active typed kill/drop objective now supplies the pull obligation. The published quest root preempts travel, the existing owned target handoff observes the exact target acknowledgement, and MountedCombatTransition owns landing and dismount before damage. A transient empty target publication does not discard a still-current required objective. Ordinary roaming independently prioritizes a known target over an unrelated hotspot and rechecks acquisition during an active pursuit. It releases the old child before acquisition; cleanup-created service work and replaced owners cannot be overwritten. Unchanged movement is not restarted every pulse.

**Naladu ground mount.** At 13:11:09 the travel estimator preferred flying, then the takeoff column was blocked. The foot departure lost its flight preference during a later review and requested Black Hawkstrider at 13:11:11. A bounded preferred-departure lifetime now keeps the walk toward open ground without inserting a ground-mount request. The new footprint must still be observed before takeoff; an unavailable flight, no-fly area or exhausted departure retains its conservative fallback.

**Zurai flight stall.** Tile `530_33_21` loaded and travel continued; the later repeated stall was at `(22.376114, 4930.0576, 136.09196)`. Progress-only flight planning required a walkable landing footprint under each air waypoint. The repair separates a measured air corridor from a landing plan. An already-flying mounted actor can cross a body-clear local corridor over water, canopy or unavailable ground mesh without claiming support. A corridor contains no landing or onward-ground-route evidence. Descent, dismount and final approach still require those independent observations. Blocked/UNKNOWN collision, unsafe route segments and replaced owners do not authorize forward motion.

**Opposing bases.** A reproducible generator combines original-client faction masks with pinned TDB335 service anchors and nearby same-faction spawns. It reviews 59 footprints on maps 530 and 571 and enables 51 unambiguous opposing settlements. Eight overlapping shared/contested footprints remain conditional rather than receiving a fabricated permanent owner. The 80-yard footprint margin is a planning policy, not a measured server aggro radius. Full derivation receipts are in `../aerial-settlements.json`; raw DBC assets remain outside the repository.

The existing aerial route manager now applies exclusions to whole segments, includes faction and exclusion revision in route ownership, and invalidates removed regions. Failed/partial avoidance paths no longer fall back to the original direct destination. Corner smoothing, collision ray alternatives, final movement and blind recovery input cannot bypass exclusions. Overlapping planning rectangles are merged before pathfinding; an actor already inside a base can route through a nearby exit. Local quest flight follows the safe route's next waypoint rather than always pointing toward the final NPC.

## Team combat and protected recipients

Consecration's actual Retribution selector, nearby-unit enumeration and shared engagement policy are tested together with party threat and no player aggro. The configured count remains meaningful: three required nearby enemies need not be attacking the paladin. Selection alone is not engagement. Existing mana reserve, cooldown, moving-target and crowd-control exclusions remain active.

Player protection is independent of temporary reaction. Hostile party members are rejected as combat targets, and all dungeon/raid players are protected. The area scan includes derived WoWPlayer wrappers before pet/owner exclusions; otherwise a charmed teammate could disappear from the scan. Shared Targeting publication and retained accessors reject protected/stale combat candidates while heal and loot providers retain their separate purpose.

Native spell admission binds the actor, exact recipient, complete raw roster and relevant player relation states before assembling the command. The final guard performs memory observations rather than nested native/Lua queries. Friendly healing, caster defense and friendly Dispel Magic remain usable. Harmful self-centered effects and active cleave/proc hazards are not mistaken for support. Owned start/pet commands validate their original-client target tokens; the shared pulse can stop an ongoing attack whose recipient becomes protected. Reaction caching now includes descriptor identity and faction/charm changes even within one frame or an outer descriptor cache.

These are dispatch and observation guarantees. They do not establish that every private-server modification is represented, or that an already-placed persistent spell can be withdrawn before a future mind-control event.

## Every-quest review

`every-quest.csv` has exactly 4,335 unique dataset quest IDs, one exclusive disposition per quest, objective families, execution drivers and unresolved reasons. The audited catalogue contains 5,771 objectives: 3,729 PrimitiveCandidate quests, 3 ImplementedStrategy quests and 603 HandlerOrSourceRequired quests. The derivation reverified 40 primary tables and 631 pinned C++ source files. Existing runtime catalogue bytes matched the derivation exactly.

PrimitiveCandidate means that the source contract can be considered by the existing primitive executor; it is not evidence of live completion. HandlerOrSourceRequired remains held. Unsupported item, gossip, scripted credit, escort, special movement and return mechanisms are not silently converted to ordinary kills. The common pickup/turn-in/kill/drop execution repairs apply across admitted quest families, including adjacent Naladu and Zurai work; no NPC-specific movement exception was added.
