Five shared Hunter trap helpers submitted hard-coded maximum-rank IDs through a void behavior-tree action. A lower-level hunter could learn the trap yet submit an unavailable rank; global cooldown/resource/readiness vetoes and local executor failures could still appear as a successful action. The selected enemy could also change during setup without retaining the original decision identity.

The existing overloads now retain one actor and candidate GUID/reference and route the named trap through the actual shared Singular Cast and SpellManager owners. They select the observed learned rank, preserve the shared readiness and local dispatch receipt, and revalidate the original candidate through yielded setup and logging callbacks. Add selection retains the existing closest engaged stationary/player policy. These are original-client traps placed at the hunter; the compatibility launcher argument still adds no Trap Launcher behavior. The existing40-yard pre-placement policy is retained, not promoted to proof that a trap can hit an enemy at that distance.
